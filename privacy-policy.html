Privacy Policy

1. Information we may receive

To process orders and provide support, the shop may receive MLBB User ID, Zone ID, in-game name, order details, payment slip images, contact information, and messages sent to the shop.

2. How information is used

This information may be used to verify payments, identify the game account, process orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and investigate order problems.

3. Service providers

Information necessary to complete an order may be shared with a third-party MLBB top-up provider or service used for order processing.

4. Account security

The shop does not need your MLBB password or OTP. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or other sensitive login credentials.

5. Retention

Order and payment information may be retained for reasonable business, support, dispute, and record-keeping purposes.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated when the shop's services or data practices change.
