Refund Policy

1. Completed digital orders

Because MLBB top-up products are digital goods, completed and successfully delivered top-ups are generally not refundable.

2. Paid but not delivered

If you paid but the digital top-up was not delivered, contact KYAW ZIN Gaming Shop with your Order ID, MLBB User ID, Zone ID, payment slip, and a description of the issue. The order can then be checked and investigated.

3. Incorrect account information

If an order was submitted with an incorrect User ID or Zone ID, recovery or refund may not be possible after processing. Please check the information carefully before ordering.

4. Duplicate payments

Duplicate payments can be reviewed when proof of both payments is provided. Any resolution depends on the payment and order records.

5. Third-party service issues

Where a third-party provider is involved, investigation may require additional processing time.
